Accessibility

Nainoia Inc wants everyone to be able to read the Holy Bible Aionian Edition®. This statement applies to the website www.AionianBible.org.

Conformance status

The pages listed under Scope below conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22. This means they fully meet every Level A and Level AA success criterion, apart from the third-party content listed under Exceptions.

Date of this claim: November 2026. Last checked: November 2026, with automated testing (axe-core, the Nu HTML Checker, EPUBCheck, and Ace by DAISY) and manual review, in light and dark mode, at phone and desktop widths, and in right-to-left languages.

Scope

  • All pages on www.AionianBible.org, including the home page, Preface, Reader's Guide, Glossary, Maps, History, Buy, Publisher, Privacy, and this page
  • All Bible pages under /Bibles, /Parallel, /Strongs, /Read, and /Verse, in every language
  • The ePub Bibles, which are separately marked as conforming to EPUB Accessibility 1.1 and WCAG 2.2 Level AA

Exceptions: statement of partial conformance for third-party content

The following content is provided by third parties and is not under our control. It is excluded from this claim under WCAG 2.2, section 5.4, Statement of Partial Conformance - Third Party Content. The same Bible text is always available in accessible form on the pages listed under Scope.

  • The image gallery at /gallery
  • The Zaya, DearFlip, and Readium book viewers at /Zaya, /DearFlip, and /Readium
  • The Google Translate tool
  • Websites we link to, such as Amazon, Lulu, Barnes&Noble, PayPal, and social media

The PDF Bibles are not yet fully accessible. The same text is available as accessible web pages and accessible ePubs, which are a conforming alternate version.

Technologies relied upon

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WAI-ARIA. The Bible text can be read without JavaScript.

Accessibility features

  • Every page and every Bible passage is marked with its language and text direction, including right-to-left languages
  • Pages use headings, landmarks, and a consistent menu, so screen readers can move around quickly
  • Text can be enlarged with the + button or the browser, and reflows on small screens
  • Colors meet WCAG contrast requirements in light and dark mode
  • Maps and illustrations have text descriptions

Feedback

If you find something you cannot read or use, please contact us from the Publisher page. Please include the page address and what went wrong. We will reply as soon as possible.
